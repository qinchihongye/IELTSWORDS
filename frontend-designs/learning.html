atmosphere

/ˈætməsfɪə(r)/

大气层；气氛

词根词缀

atmo- (蒸汽) + -sphere (球体) → 包围地球的气体层

派生词

atmospheric (adj. 大气的) atmospherics (n. 大气现象)

例句

The atmosphere in the meeting room was tense as everyone waited for the results.
会议室里的气氛很紧张，每个人都在等待结果。

候选词

ambience environment mood
